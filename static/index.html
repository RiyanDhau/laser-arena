      📖 Welcome to Jhare Shot — the Rulebook

      Be the last player standing. Everybody hides, aims and fires at the same moment — so think like your opponents!

      🎯 How a round works

      1. Place — click or tap anywhere in the arena to put your character down. Nobody can see where anyone else stands.
      2. Aim — move the mouse (or tap / drag on a phone) to choose the direction you face. Your laser fires that way.
      3. Lock in — press Lock in & fire (or Enter). You have 40 seconds; if you don't lock in, you're out.
      4. Fire! — once everyone has locked in, all lasers fire at the same time. Anyone a laser hits is eliminated (a laser stops at the first player it hits). Two players aiming at each other both go down.
      5. Survivors play the next round. The last player standing wins; if everyone is eliminated, it's a draw. Eliminated players keep watching and chatting.

      ✨ Hidden power-ups (from round 4)

      Power-ups are hidden in the arena. You are only told how many there are — never where. After each shot they are all revealed.

      • How many: rounds 4–10 have 1, rounds 11–20 have 2, rounds 21–30 have 3 — and one more every 10 rounds.
      • Activate one: place your character within 50 px of a hidden power-up and it's yours for that round's shot. If several players are on the same one, the closest wins.
      • Earn a shield: if your beam touches a power-up icon (other than the one you're standing on) you earn a 🛡 Shield.
      🔦 Torch
      Your laser becomes a triangle 30° wide that hits everyone inside it.
      🪞 Reflector
      Your beam bounces off the walls 3 times. Careful: it can come back and hit you!
      🛡 Shield
      Earned by touching an icon with your beam. It protects you during the next round only: it absorbs every hit on you that round, then expires.
      ↪ Deflector (from round 11)
      A hidden bumper: any beam that touches it turns 90°. It can't be picked up, and the Torch's wide cone isn't bent.
      💣 Bomber (rounds 10, 20, 30…)
      Always hidden in one of 5 spots: the four corners or the centre — you know the spots, not which one it is. Stand on it and in the next round you get a bomb: after aiming, press 💣 Place bomb and click anywhere. When the lasers fire it explodes (radius 90 px) and eliminates every other player whose character touches the blast. A shield can absorb it. Everyone sees who holds a bomb — nobody sees where it lands.

      💡 Tips

      • Use the chat to bluff, make deals, or beg for mercy.
      • The corners and the centre are tempting on bomber rounds… but everyone knows it.
      • Don't aim a Reflector straight at a wall — it bounces right back at you.
      • 🎵 Music too loud? Use the speaker button at the bottom right, or press M to mute.
      • A room holds 2–10 players.
